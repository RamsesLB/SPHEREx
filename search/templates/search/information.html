{% blocktrans %}The same search address can return the results as JSON. Add format=json to the URL, or send the header Accept: application/json. The response looks in the local database and then waits for IRSA. If IRSA answers, those observations are returned. If IRSA fails, the saved observations are returned.{% endblocktrans %}

SPHEREx

{% blocktrans %}SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization, and Ices Explorer) is a NASA mission led by the Jet Propulsion Laboratory and Caltech. It surveys the whole sky in near-infrared light and records a spectrum at every point. The images used here come from the public archive at IRSA.{% endblocktrans %}
